About the role

Storyblok, a headless CMS company, is hiring a Senior Product Architect to own the technical architecture, platform vision and scalability of its products. The role involves setting architectural guardrails, leading architecture reviews and working with Product, Security, DevOps and Data teams. It requires 7+ years in software architecture or system design for large-scale distributed cloud applications, with deep cloud-native expertise (AWS preferred). The company is fully remote and offers a home office package, a monthly remote work stipend, 25 days of annual leave and a personal development fund.
