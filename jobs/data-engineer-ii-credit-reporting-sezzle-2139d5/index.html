About the role

Sezzle is hiring a Data Engineer II to own the data pipelines and reporting processes that send customer account information to credit bureaus. The role involves building and debugging SQL and Python workflows, tracing and fixing reporting discrepancies, and adding automated tests and monitoring. The engineer will work with Customer Service on dispute escalations, with Compliance on reporting rules, and with Engineering on production defects.
