About the role

Coalition Technologies' CT Marketing Agency is hiring freelance copywriters for project-based work producing SEO-driven content for eCommerce and lead generation websites, such as category pages, product descriptions, blog posts and landing pages. Candidates need excellent English writing skills, research ability and a portfolio of published work, with 2 to 4 years of professional writing experience preferred. Pay is per word, and selected writers complete a brief training course after an offer.
