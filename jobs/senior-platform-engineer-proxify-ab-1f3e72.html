About the role

Proxify is recruiting a Senior Platform Engineer for one of its clients to build and run an internal developer platform that lets product teams deploy and manage infrastructure on their own. The role calls for 5+ years in platform engineering, SRE or DevOps, with strong Kubernetes, GitOps (ArgoCD/Flux), Terraform-style infrastructure as code, and Go or Python skills. Experience with developer portals like Backstage and with observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana and OpenTelemetry is also expected. The listing says the position is remote with no stated region restriction, so it appears open to applicants in Kenya, though the client is not named.
