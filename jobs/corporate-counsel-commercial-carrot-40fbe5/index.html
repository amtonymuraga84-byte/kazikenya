About the role

Carrot, a global fertility and family care platform, is hiring a Corporate Counsel to negotiate, draft and review customer and vendor contracts such as master services agreements, SLAs, professional services agreements and NDAs. The role partners with Sales and other teams to resolve redlines, advises on legal and revenue risk, and supports privacy terms including HIPAA Business Associate Agreements and Data Protection Agreements. It sits within a collaborative Legal team and uses contract lifecycle management and AI tools.
