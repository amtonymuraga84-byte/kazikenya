Public Service Internship Programme (PSIP) – Apply Online

Public Service Commission of Kenya · Other Kenya · 1 day ago
InternshipOther

The Public Service Commission (PSC) places Kenyan graduates in ministries, state departments and other public bodies under the Public Service Internship Programme. According to the Commission's guidelines, interns are unemployed graduates from recognised institutions, the internship lasts 12 months, and interns receive a monthly stipend. How to apply: applications are made online on the PSC recruitment portal using the Apply button. Each advert lists its own positions, eligibility and closing date, so read the current advert carefully before you submit. Documents to prepare: national ID, degree or diploma certificate and transcript, KRA PIN and a certificate of good conduct. Applying is free, so never pay anyone to be considered.
