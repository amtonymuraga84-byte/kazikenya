Addressing attendees during a national prayer and thanksgiving service on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, First Lady Rachel Ruto offered insight into how the Head of State manages his demanding itinerary. Responding to inquiries from citizens curious about his relentless drive, she highlighted that spiritual fortitude plays a central role in sustaining his dedication to public service.

"I thank God very much for our President. He is a man who has dedicated himself to serving Kenyans. While some people wonder how he never seems to get tired, I believe it is because of the spirit God has given him to serve Kenyans."

Rachel Ruto underscored the importance of prayer and divine guidance within their family and administration. She encouraged citizens to continue praying for the country and supporting its leadership as the government works toward fulfilling its economic growth objectives and national development agenda.
