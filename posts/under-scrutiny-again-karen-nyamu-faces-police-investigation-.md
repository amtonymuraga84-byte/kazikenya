In a statement issued on October 24, 2026, the National Police Service (NPS) publicly addressed video footage showing Nominated Senator Karen Nyamu discussing recruitment irregularities. The police service described her assertions as "uncalled for, irresponsible, and unacceptable," confirming that an investigation is underway to establish facts and determine whether criminal liability exists.   

During a media interview, Nyamu recounted assisting loyal campaign volunteers who lacked the necessary academic qualifications to secure spots in the police service. She stated:   

"I can even tell you of a recent case where I tried to help someone get into the police force, a very staunch campaigner of mine; they are even here... So I said (we bargained) let them clear his conditions, because I had this chance and I really wanted to give back."   

According to Nyamu, the candidate made it to Kiganjo Police Training College before authorities discovered forged documentation, leading to the individual's dismissal alongside eight others. While the police service acknowledged previous reports of fraudulent academic certificates during recruitment drives, officials noted that Nyamu’s remarks did not automatically establish personal guilt.

This latest controversy adds to a growing list of public incidents involving the lawmaker:   

Senate Reprimand (March 2025): Senate Speaker Amason Kingi directed Nyamu to issue an unreserved apology after she made inappropriate remarks to a Grade 10 student visiting Parliament. On May 8, 2025, she apologized, acknowledging her comments fell below the dignity expected of a public officer.   

Finance Bill Backlash: Nyamu faced significant online criticism over her vocal defense of the Finance Bill 2024. She later clarified that her intention was not to disrespect struggling youth, expressing regret to those offended.   

Public Heckling (September 2026): Attending an event hosted by media personality Sample Cutz at the KICC, Nyamu was heckled by members of the crowd chanting opposition slogans. She later stated that she was unbothered by the reaction and would continue her legislative work. 

The situation raises broader questions regarding political influence and the integrity of public institutions. While supporters may view her comments as an honest reflection of political patronage, critics argue that political loyalty cannot justify altering recruitment standards for national security services.   

Police investigations remain ongoing to establish whether the claims warrant formal legal action.
