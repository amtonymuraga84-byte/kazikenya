Your **KRA PIN** (Personal Identification Number) is your tax identity in Kenya. Employers need it to pay you and deduct PAYE, and you will be asked for it when you open a bank account, apply for HELB, register a business, buy land or a car, or apply for a government job. It is an 11-character number that starts with the letter **A**.

The good news: you can register online on KRA's iTax portal, and residents receive their PIN certificate straight away.

## What you need

According to KRA, Kenyan residents registering as individuals need:

- Their **national ID** details
- Their **employer's PIN**, if they are employed
- Their **business registration certificate** details, if they run a registered business

You will also need an **email address** you can access, because iTax sends a one-time code (OTP) and your PIN details to it. Use a personal email you will keep for years; you will need it to log in to iTax every June when filing returns.

## Step-by-step: registering on iTax

1. **Go to the official iTax portal** at itax.kra.go.ke.
2. **Choose "New PIN Registration".**
3. **Select "Individual"** as the taxpayer type and **"Online Form"** as the registration mode.
4. **Fill in the form.** For Kenyan citizens, some details fill in automatically once you enter your ID number and date of birth. Check that your names and date of birth match your ID exactly.
5. **Add your income details**, such as your employer's PIN if you are employed.
6. **Verify your email** with the OTP sent to you, then submit.
7. **Download your PIN certificate.** It appears as soon as your application is confirmed, and a copy is sent to your email. Save the PDF somewhere safe.

## New in 2026: a PIN for students and people with no income

On April 18, 2026, KRA introduced a **PIN Without Obligation (PWO)**. It is designed for people who need a PIN for official purposes but do not earn taxable income, such as university students applying for HELB loans.

The difference is important: **PWO holders get a PIN but do not have to file tax returns.** Before this, many students got a regular PIN, never filed nil returns, and later found penalties waiting for them when they got their first job.

To apply, follow the same steps above, but choose **"Yes"** when asked whether you want a PIN without obligation. You will need your national ID, an email address for the OTP, your physical and postal address, and a reason for applying.

**When you start earning,** for example when you get your first job, you must update your PIN on iTax to add the right tax obligation (usually income tax for employees).

## Common questions

**Do I have to pay for a KRA PIN?** Registration on iTax does not have a KRA fee. If you use a cyber café, you only pay them for their time and printing.

**I have forgotten my iTax password.** Use the "Forgot password" option on the iTax login page; a reset link is sent to the email linked to your PIN.

**I lost my PIN certificate.** Log in to iTax and reprint it from your account.

**Do I need to file returns?** If you have a regular PIN, yes, every year by June 30, even if you earned nothing (a "nil" return). If you have a PIN Without Obligation, no, until you add a tax obligation.
