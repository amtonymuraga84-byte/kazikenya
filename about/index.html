About RemoteKazi

RemoteKazi is a free job site for people in Kenya. We list vacancies across the country and remote roles open to Kenyan applicants, and we publish practical guides on CVs, interviews, salaries and the documents employers ask for.

Why we started

Looking for work in Kenya often means scrolling through WhatsApp groups and Facebook pages full of expired adverts, copied posts and outright scams that ask for a “processing fee”. RemoteKazi was started to give job seekers one clean place to find genuine openings, with every listing linking straight to the employer's own page.

Who runs RemoteKazi

RemoteKazi was founded and is run by Cherop Antony Muigai, a mechanical engineer based in Nairobi. Antony holds a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT) and has worked in manufacturing, equipment maintenance and site supervision on large residential projects.

Having gone through the Kenyan job market himself, from university to the factory floor and construction sites, he built RemoteKazi around the questions job seekers actually ask: where are the real jobs, what do they pay, and how do I avoid being scammed?

How listings are collected and checked

  • Sources: we gather vacancies from public job feeds, job boards that allow reuse, and employers who send us their adverts.
  • Screening: software keeps only jobs open to people in Kenya, writes a short plain-language summary, and flags warning signs such as fees, personal-email-only contact or unrealistic pay. Only low-risk listings are published.
  • Freshness: listings are refreshed daily and removed at their closing date, or after 14 days if no date is given.
  • Apply at the source: every job links to the original advert, where you apply. We never collect applications or CVs.

Automated summaries can contain mistakes, so always confirm the details on the employer's page.

Our articles

Our blog and guides are written in our own words and focus on careers, salaries, money and workplace rights in Kenya. When an article is based on news or research, we link to the original sources at the end. Images on the site are our own illustrations unless a credit says otherwise. If you spot an error, email us and we will correct it.

Free for job seekers

RemoteKazi is free to use. The site is supported by advertising and by featured listings for employers, which are clearly labelled. We will never ask you for money to apply for a job, and no genuine employer will either. Read how to spot a fake job advert.
