About RemoteKazi

RemoteKazi helps people in Kenya find jobs in Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu and beyond, plus remote roles open to applicants in Kenya. Browsing is free.

How listings are collected

We gather vacancies from public job feeds and partner sources. Software summarises each listing in plain language, keeps only jobs relevant to Kenya, and flags adverts that look like scams. Every listing links to the original page, where you apply. Summaries can contain mistakes, so always check the original advert.

How we make money

The site shows advertising and may carry sponsored listings, which are labelled. We never charge job seekers.
