Publish a post

Fill the form, add a photo and click Publish. Your post goes live in about 1 to 2 minutes.

1. One-time setup (your GitHub key)

Create a key once: GitHub → your profile picture → Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens → Generate new token. Pick only this repository, and set Contents: Read and write and Actions: Read and write. Paste it below. It stays in this browser only and is never sent anywhere except GitHub. Never share this key with anyone, including me.

2. Write your post

A future date schedules the post. It appears on that day after the daily update.

Two or three short sentences. Put **two stars** around words to make them bold.

Use your own photo or a free one from Unsplash or Pexels. It is shrunk automatically to load fast.

Keep it short and easy to scan: aim for 300 to 500 words. Check every fee and date on the official website first.
