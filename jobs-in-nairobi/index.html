Nairobi has more formal jobs than any other county in Kenya. Banks, telecoms, tech start-ups, manufacturers, the UN and hundreds of NGOs all have offices here, alongside government ministries and parastatals.

Tips for Nairobi job seekers: factor commuting time and cost into any offer, since a job across town can cost you several hours and a large share of your pay each week. Many employers in Upper Hill, Westlands and Industrial Area shortlist online, so keep your CV short, clear and tailored to each advert.
