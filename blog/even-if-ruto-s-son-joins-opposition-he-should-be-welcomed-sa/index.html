Even If Ruto's Son Joins Opposition, He Should Be Welcomed," Says Ndindi Nyoro

Published 21 Oct 2026 · By RemoteKazi Team · 1 min read

Kiharu MP Ndindi Nyoro has sparked political debate after stating that anyone willing to join the opposition—even President William Ruto’s own son—should be embraced in the effort to unseat the current administration.

Speaking during a recent political rally, Kiharu Member of Parliament Ndindi Nyoro declared that the opposition must remain open-minded about potential allies. He stated, "Even if Ruto's own son ditches him and joins opposition, he should be given a chance so that we can kick Ruto out."

The statement has triggered mixed reactions across social media platforms, with many observers interpreting it as a signal that opposition figures are willing to build broad alliances with anyone ready to challenge the sitting government.

Nyoro, previously a close ally of President William Ruto, has noticeably shifted his political stance in recent months. He has increasingly aligned himself with leaders critical of the Kenya Kwanza administration, indicating growing factionalism and changing loyalties within the country’s political landscape.

His comments highlight a strategy focused on broad inclusivity and strength in numbers, even if that entails welcoming individuals previously situated within President Ruto’s immediate inner circle.
